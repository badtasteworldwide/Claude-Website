# Refund policy

Paste the text below into **Shopify admin → Settings → Policies → Refund policy** (replacing
the current text), then Save. It keeps your existing terms (buyer pays return shipping, free
replacement for damaged/defective items) and adds the 30-day window.

---

**30-day returns**

You can return unused frames in their original condition within 30 days of delivery. To start a return, email badtasteworldwide@gmail.com (or use our contact page) with your order number. We'll reply with the return address.

Custom and personalized orders are made just for you and can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or wrong.

**Damaged, defective or wrong items**

If your order arrives damaged, defective or isn't what you ordered, email us a photo within 14 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement free of charge. No need to send the original back unless we ask.

**Return shipping**

You're responsible for the cost of shipping a return back to us, and original shipping charges aren't refundable. For returns over $75, we recommend a trackable shipping service or shipping insurance, since we can't guarantee we'll receive returned items.

**Refunds**

Once your return arrives and is inspected, we'll email you to confirm whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5-10 business days; your bank or card issuer may take a little longer to post it.

**Late or missing refunds**

If you haven't received your refund, check your bank account again, then contact your card company or bank, since posting can take some time. If you've done that and still don't see it, email us at badtasteworldwide@gmail.com.

**Exchanges**

We replace items that are defective or damaged. For anything else, return the item for a refund and place a new order.

**EU and UK customers**

You also have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. If you cancel, we'll refund the item price and the original standard shipping cost; you pay the cost of sending the item back.
