Every Bad Taste frame ships from our studio in Fremont, California.

**Processing time**

Orders ship within 1-3 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding US holidays). Custom orders and preorders ship on the timeline listed on their product page. You'll get an email with tracking as soon as your order ships.

If we can't ship within that time, we'll email you a new estimate and give you the option to cancel for a full refund.

**US shipping**

- We ship with USPS. Rates are calculated at checkout based on your order and address.
- Free US shipping on orders with $40 or more in license plate frames. Only plate frames count toward the $40; stickers, shirts and other items don't.
- USPS delivery usually takes 2-5 business days after your order ships, depending on the service you choose at checkout.

**International shipping**

- We ship worldwide with USPS and DHL Express. Rates are calculated at checkout.
- Delivery usually takes 7-21 business days with USPS or 3-7 business days with DHL Express after your order ships. Customs can add time.
- Import duties, taxes and customs fees are not included in your order total. They're set by your country and paid by you on delivery.
- If a package is refused or its duties go unpaid, we'll refund the item price once it's returned to us, minus the original shipping cost and any return charges.

Delivery times are carrier estimates, not guarantees.

**Shipping address**

Please double-check your address at checkout. We can't change it after your order ships. If a package comes back to us as undeliverable because of an incorrect address, we can reship it for the cost of shipping or refund the item price minus shipping.

**Lost or stolen packages**

If tracking shows your package as delivered but you can't find it, check with neighbors and your local post office first. Then contact us within 14 days of the delivery date and we'll help you file a claim with the carrier. Replacements are at our discretion; we're not responsible for packages marked delivered to the address provided at checkout.

**Damaged or wrong items**

If your frame arrives damaged or we sent the wrong item, email us a photo within 14 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement free of charge. For everything else, see our Refund Policy.

**Questions**

Email badtasteworldwide@gmail.com or use our contact page at badtaste.world/pages/contact. Include your order number and we'll get back to you.
